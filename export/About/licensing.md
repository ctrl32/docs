# Licensing

**ctrl32 is free** — the editor and the runtimes for **ESP32**, **STM32**
and **Linux** devices, for home, school and commercial use alike, on any
number of computers and any number of devices. Download the editor from
the [download page](https://ctrl32.com/download/) and everything in it
works.

---

## The terms

ctrl32 is provided under its end-user licence agreement, the
[EULA](https://ctrl32.com/eula/). It is the one document that says what
you may do with the editor and the runtimes and what the software does
not promise; read it before you put ctrl32 into a machine.

**Every version ships everything the editor has** — [ladder](/docs/programming/ladder/),
FBD and [ST](/docs/programming/st-language/), [WEBview](/docs/visualisation/webview/) and
[HMIview](/docs/visualisation/hmiview/), [Modbus](/docs/buses/modbus/), [MQTT](/docs/buses/mqtt/) and
CAN/[CANopen](/docs/buses/canopen/), the **[Linux runtime](/docs/hardware/linux-runtime/)**
with OPC UA and the ten-times limits, online monitoring, the device
library.

**Your projects are yours.** Nothing is locked, deleted or encrypted,
and the boards you have downloaded into keep running.

## Third-party components

ctrl32 is built on open-source components, each under its own licence.
They are listed, with their licences and what each one does, on
